# SmartLogistics Company Policy

## Standard Delivery

Standard delivery has a target delivery period of five calendar days.
This is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date.
Customers should receive updates when a delivery is delayed.

## Express Delivery

Express delivery has a target delivery period of two calendar days.
This is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date.
Express orders should be prioritized during shipment processing.

## Delayed Deliveries

When a shipment is overdue, customer support should check its
latest recorded status and contact the operations team for an update.
The customer should receive a clear explanation when verified
information is available.
Staff must not invent delivery dates or promise an unconfirmed arrival.

## Damaged Parcels

When a customer reports a damaged parcel, staff should record the
shipment ID, the customer's description of the damage, and the date
the issue was reported.
The issue should be referred to the operations team for investigation.
Staff should not promise compensation unless it has been approved
under the company's actual compensation policy.

## Missing Shipments

When a shipment cannot be located, staff should verify the shipment ID
and check the latest available tracking record.
If the parcel remains unlocated, the issue should be escalated to
the operations team.
Only verified tracking information should be shared with customers.

## Customer Complaints

Customer complaints should be recorded with the shipment ID,
the issue reported, and the date received.
Staff should communicate politely and explain the next steps.
Sensitive customer information should only be shared with authorized
staff who need it to handle the complaint.
